## Kernel side
- The i915 uapi `errors` counter went from 16 to 140 across the two hardware runs, +124 for 120
  pictures, so about 1 per picture. `last -`, and nothing is logged at
  uapi_log=1, so every one is EINVAL, ENODEV or ENOENT.
- exec_vcs stayed 0, so no submission reached vcs0.
- There is no GPU hang, reset or ESR line in the klog.
- Most likely cause, not yet confirmed: iHD's per-frame `DRM_IOCTL_I915_GEM_EXECBUFFER2` on
  Gen9 uses relocations, and `uapi_abi.rs:673` rejects them
  (`relocation_count != 0 -> EINVAL`, softpin only). To confirm, boot once with
  `i915.uapi_log=2` and look for `ioctl 0x29 ... -> -22`. That needs a
  cmdline that keeps the Wi-Fi args (`espctl cmdline trial ...` built
  from the existing args on the guest, never printed), or a runtime knob.
- Next step (P6): either support relocations on execbuffer2 (patch the
  batch and objects at submit), or check whether iHD can be made to use
  softpin on KBL. It enables softpin only when the kernel reports
  HAS_EXEC_SOFTPIN and the platform opts in.
