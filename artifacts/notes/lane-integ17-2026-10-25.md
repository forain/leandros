## openbugs #76 (net-e2e failures seen by lane hostrace): load, not regression
On 24586177 both arches pass the whole net-e2e suite (netroamtest, netidletest, linkmontest 5 cases, nettxtest x2, x86 inloadtest incl. serial case) when run with no other test of this lane running. No bisect needed. The earlier failures coincided with heavy parallel lane load on the Mac; x86 net tests are the slowest (netroamtest 165 s, inloadtest 115 s, linkmontest 74 s vs 129/110/46 s on aarch64), so they are the first to time out under contention.

## x86_64 wifisimtest in regress (harness artifact)
First x86 regress run: wifisimtest booted `arch=aarch64` and failed (no prompt). wifisimtest.py picks its arch from the running QEMU process (`running_arch()`), and my aarch64 chain was running concurrently, so it chose aarch64. Rerun alone: PASS (34 s). Follow-up: runtests should pass its arch to wifisimtest explicitly so concurrent mixed-arch runs cannot confuse it.
