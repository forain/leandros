## 10 s 1080p30 VP9 (testsrc2, libvpx realtime, 4 Mb/s, 300 frames), ffmpeg -benchmark
| path | wall | user | sys | exec_vcs | busy_vcs_ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW (-pix_fmt nv12, -f null) | 0.50-0.53 s | 2.3-2.6 s | 0.05 s | - | - |
| VA-API + hwdownload nv12 | 2.57 s | 2.43 s | 0.22 s | +305 | +813 |
| VA-API, no download (decode only) | 0.30 s | 0.09 s | 0.14 s | +305 | +282 |

Decode alone: about 10x less CPU than SW. The hwdownload path costs about 2.3 s of user CPU
(~7.7 ms per 3 MB frame, so the CPU reads back from an uncached/WC mapping). That is a
userspace-copy cost, not a decode cost; a zero-copy consumer (dmabuf export to
a compositor or GL) avoids it. The testsrc2 clip is very easy for libvpx, so the SW wall time is optimistic.
