## Refuted premises (remove from TODO/memory)
- "No libcosmic/iced app paints a pixel" — FALSE; cosmic-settings, cosmic-app-library, panel applets all render. It was never the gate on the greeter.
- "Zink desktop runs at 0.65 fps with 2 s input stalls" — it rendered 0 frames; now 20 fps.
- "Keybindings don't fire = input loss" — input/xkb/config were fine; the bound action's fork deadlocked the compositor.
- "The snd-validation whole-guest wedge" — not reproduced 27/27; the monitor-based liveness recipe manufactured it.
- "Guest clock slow = HVF bug" — worse on TCG; all accelerators.
- "`tmpfile_owner_of` keyed by raw PID" — already fixed.
- "`waittest wait_on_process_group` is a kernel flake" — a race in the TEST (child `setpgid(0,0)` vs parent `waitpid(-pid)`); fixed in lane/timers.
- drmsmoke KMS cases FAIL on the default boot — EXPECTED: the greeter's compositor holds DRM master (EACCES); not a regression (lane/drmsmoke makes it SKIP with a diagnosis).
- Every wall-time number measured before 2026-09-16 (700 s x86 settle, 140 s HVF desktop, MAME 59 s = 69–76 s, sleep 1 = 1.3 s, WDOG intervals, "composites after 10 min", 57 s SetEnv outlier, 42 %/1.9 ms/93 ms ctrlq — that one also 4.49× too fast via `rdtsc/1000` `monotonic_us` on x86_64).
