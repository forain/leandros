# lane/ns0gen 2026-11-13 - FIXED

ns-0 mount identity for dirfds was a hash of the mountpoint path, so an fd opened under a ro bind picked up the flags of a later mount at the same path.
Fix (servers/vfs/src/mnt.rs): each ns-0 mount (alias or self-bind) gets a unique monotonic generation id (`next_id`), fds capture it; a replaced/removed flagged mount moves to a bounded DETACHED table (256, oldest dropped) so its fds keep their flags.
Test: mnttest e7_ns0_dirfd_detached_keeps_flags - red on unfixed (openat errno=0), green after (aarch64).
