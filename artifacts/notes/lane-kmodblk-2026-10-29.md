# lane/kmodblk (W2-3): block device kapi, NVMe and virtio-blk as loadable modules

Status: DELIVERED (merged into main after integrating kmodnetsnd and bkperm).

## What
- Block kapi class (`lib/kmod-abi/src/blk.rs`, `kernel/src/kmod/exports/blk.rs`): `kapi_blk_register`, `kapi_blk_remove`,
  `kapi_preempt_disabled`, `kapi_preempt_enable_no_resched`, `kapi_mutex_trylock`.
- NVME and VIRTIO_BLK are tristate (`modules/nvme`, `modules/virtio-blk`, listed in `modules/BOOT`, virtio_blk first).
- Tests: `scripts/kmod/autoloadtest.sh <arch> virtio_blk|nvme`, `scripts/kmod/kexecblktest.sh` (runtests "kexecblk").

## Merge with main (kmodnetsnd + bkperm)
- `RegKind`: SND=10 (main), BLK moved to 11.
- KSYMTAB holds both sets; NEXPORTS = 83 (78 main + 5 blk), counted from the table; ktest checks 83 plus the blk bad-arg cases.
- Duplicate `kmod_rt::pci_class` (both lanes added it) reduced to one.
- Cargo.lock taken from main and regenerated by cargo; both module sets are workspace members.
- autoloadtest.sh carries the blk, snd, hda and net cases.
- The nvme negative case no longer asserts "root wait gave up": the lx root image is a separate virtio disk, so
  blacklisting nvme does not remove the root (virtio_blk still asserts it).
