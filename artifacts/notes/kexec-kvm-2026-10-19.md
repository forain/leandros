## Chain (clean run, fresh boot)
`kexec -l /boot/kexec --initrd=/boot/kexec-initrd --reuse-cmdline --append=kexec.gen=N`,
then `kexec -e` (gen1), `kexec -e -c 2` (gen2), `kexec -e -c 3` (gen3).

| gen | started from | login | nproc | APIC | MemTotal | /proc/cmdline | prevkexec |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (Limine) | - | yes | 4 | xAPIC | 6203836 kB | (empty) | - |
| 1 | cpu0 (BSP) | yes | 4 | xAPIC | 6255824 kB | `kexec.gen=1` | 24 lines |
| 2 | cpu2 (AP, NMI to BSP) | yes | 4 | xAPIC | 6255824 kB | `kexec.gen=2` | 24 lines |
| 3 | cpu3 (AP, NMI to BSP) | yes | 4 | xAPIC | 6255824 kB | `kexec.gen=3` | 24 lines |

Every gen: `[SMP] 4 CPUs online (xAPIC)`, `BSP cpu0: INIT sent to 3 CPU(s)`, 9 virtio
functions status-reset, no panic. An accidental earlier session also chained 9 kexecs
back to back on one VM (start reused the running VM) with the same results.
KVM needed no kernel change: the x2APIC path was not exercised because OVMF/KVM leaves
the APIC in xAPIC mode with 4 vCPUs (APIC IDs 0-3).

## Regress under KVM
`runtests.py x86_64 kvm --suite regress`: 34/35 PASS. The kexec load+unload entry
passed. One failure: `sshsmoke` step `ssh_hostkey` (`ssh-keyscan`: "Connection closed by
remote host"). The other 4 ssh steps passed in the same run. On a fresh KVM boot,
`ssh-keyscan -t ed25519` (OpenSSH 10.5p1) then succeeded 4/4 against the same image, so
this was a transient flake, not a KVM or kexec bug. Not investigated further.

## Driver gotchas
- `pgrep 'qemu-system-x86_6[4]'` without `-f` never matches (comm is truncated to 15
  chars). Use `pgrep -f`.
- `driver.py start` reuses a VM that is already running under the same run id.
- The guest has no `grep`; use `head`/`cat` in checks.
