- **Interrupt paths**: virtio-input, virtio-net, virtio-gpu control queue and virtio-blk (hybrid: spin `virtio_blk.spin_us`, default 50 us, then park on the completion IRQ) are interrupt-driven. virtio-blk wait statistics (spins that finished the request, parks, rechecks, IRQ vs non-IRQ wakeups) are the "virtio-blk wait:" line of `/proc/interrupts`; x86_64 KVM box, dd 256 MiB conv=fsync, interleaved modes: hybrid 19-27 MB/s (mean 24), park 16-26 (22), spin 26-27 (27); lane/blkhyb's "hybrid 2x slower than park and spin" does not reproduce (no lost wakeup: a non-IRQ wake finds the request complete on 1.8% of waits, the same as pure park), so keep the hybrid default. Reference (aarch64 HVF dd 256 MiB conv=fsync, qemu CPU-s per 768 MiB: hybrid ~3.2, pure spin ~6.1, pure park ~4.1; throughput hybrid >= spin > park, noisy on a shared Mac); virtio-snd TX stays tick-pumped (the 100 Hz pump must run anyway, an IRQ gains little).
- **Synthetic sysfs**: `/sys/class/input`, `/sys/dev/char` (13:* are symlinks to the class entries; 226:* are staged symlinks into `/sys/devices/pci.../drm/cardN`), `/sys/class/drm` (card0/renderD128 are staged links to `/sys/devices/.../drm/<node>`; listing also shows the connectors) and the `card0-<TYPE>-<n>/{status,enabled,modes,dpms,edid}` connector files are generated (`servers/vfs/src/sysinput.rs`, `sysdrm.rs`, `sysclass.rs`; symlinks via `sysclass::link/expand`; the DRM tree itself is staged by `scripts/mkfs-f2fs-populated.py` with real `drm/<node>` dirs holding `device`/`subsystem` links; gated by the `sysfs` suite `sys_*`/`drm_*`/`input_*`). Still open: `properties`/`ff` details follow EVIOCGBIT only; `edid` is a zero-length file; everything outside `/sys/class/block`, `/sys/class/input`, `/sys/class/drm`, `/sys/dev/char` stays disk-staged.
- **VT console glyphs**: double-width characters (CJK, emoji) occupy two cells but draw as a placeholder box; the console font is ASCII-only (no CJK/emoji/Unicode Latin: precomposed Latin-1 letters and combining marks U+0300..0327 are drawn as ASCII base plus an overlaid accent). Marks without a bitmap, or after a column-0 wrap, are ignored. Unifont (GPLv2+FE) is not vendored; coverage stays a gap.
- **sshd**: privilege separation P1-P4 are done: vendored russh 0.64.1 (`userland/sshd/vendor/russh`, signer hook, `auth_publickey_signed`), the listener re-execs one `sshd -R` per connection (crash isolation, MaxStartups, LoginGraceTime kill), and each connection is split into a root monitor plus a uid 22 net child (chroot /var/empty, NNP, seccomp kill filter incl. socketpair and mlock/munlock, no host-key fd), gated by the sshsmoke `privsep_*` steps. Docs done (`docs/design/sshd-privsep.md`). The stalled-monitor listener backstop, `close_range` and RSS/throughput numbers are done. Not implemented: `-D`/streamlocal specific handling, `AuthorizedPrincipalsFile`, `RevokedKeys`, `Match Host/LocalAddress/RDomain`. `environment=` with `PermitUserEnvironment yes` and the `$sha256$` rehash write path of a read-only `/etc` are unverified live (the `yes` case needs a second sshd config). Still open: after the pre-auth `sshd -R` child is killed from outside (SEGV or KILL, while a login session is also live) a raw client socket never sees EOF (not within 28 s), although the process is gone, the listener is unchanged and new logins work (sshsmoke `privsep_crash_isolated` reports `probe_eof=False` as KNOWN-OPEN, does not gate). Not the accept path: the listener drops its accepted-socket copy right after spawn, and nettest `tcp_kill_closes*` (fork, SIGKILL/SEGV, extra thread, thread blocked in epoll_wait) all pass, so the kernel closes plain sockets of killed processes. Evidence from /proc/<listener>/fd after the failure: the listener still holds an unexpected extra socketpair end (fd 14) and 3 pidfds, i.e. the victim's `spawn_child` task never finished; suspect the dead child is not reaped (tokio pidfd/SIGCHLD wake missing for an externally killed child) and its fd table survives as a zombie until reaped. Next: print the victim's /proc/<pid>/status State and the listener's waitpid result after the kill.

## Notes (not work items: by design, upstream-blocked, out of scope, or deferred by the user)

- **xdg-desktop-portal Screenshot needs a permission-store grant** (by design): non-interactive Screenshot works once the permission store holds a "screenshot" grant for the caller (probe grants it; capture verified, 1280x800 PNG in 148 ms). Without a grant xdg-desktop-portal shows Access.AccessDialog and cosmic's dialog is never answered headless (response 2 after 25 s) - by design, not a bug. (timedate1, SwitcherooControl and the a11y bus are activatable since lane portaldbus.)
- **virtio-snd TX is reaped by the 100 Hz tick pump, not an interrupt** (by design): the pump must run anyway to top the queue up with silence (QEMU 11.x loses the refill wakeup for good on an empty stream queue), so an IRQ would add nothing.
- **smoltcp "handle does not refer to a valid socket" panic (watch)**: hardened at the source (lane smolpanic2: vendored SocketSet get/get_mut resolve a stale handle to an inert stand-in, remove() of one is a no-op; brtest `stale_race` guards it); the 2026-11-14 recurrence on lane/ip6dnat was never reproduced. If a panic with this text shows again, the stale access is in a caller that kept a handle across a stack reap.
- **Interrupt-context serial diagnostics can drop under back-pressure** (by design).
- **fd-limit fence loss**: EMFILE in a virgl client's submit kills its context (as Linux); fd headroom is the defence.
- **Host-refused `RING_IDX` submit is never completed by QEMU** (descriptors lost); Mesa cannot reach it. Do not rewrite RING_IDX kernel-side.
- **Laptop SSH came up late on two 2026-10 boots** (not reproduced in ten later boots; watch).
